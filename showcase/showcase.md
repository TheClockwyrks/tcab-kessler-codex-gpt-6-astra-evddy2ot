# Make room in the sky.

**Kessler** is an orbital demolition arcade game about keeping one small planet safe beneath a very crowded sky. Ride a deflector around its atmosphere and send a spinning demolition ball into three belts of abandoned hardware.

Every return is a choice. Meet the ball squarely for a direct shot, or catch it near the tip of your deflector to send it across the orbit. The belts turn in opposite directions, armored derelicts take a second hit, and the containment field sends stray shots back into the mess.

Catch what falls. Salvage can widen your deflector, split the action into multiple balls, raise a one-use shield, or let every shot pierce straight through metal. A red pod narrows your safety net instead. Reaching for a useful tool is only worth it if you can still make the next save.

Clear the sky, collect your bonus, and face a faster wave. You have three lives, six balls at most, and as much orbit as you can handle.
